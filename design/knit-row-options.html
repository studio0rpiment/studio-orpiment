Knit row options

Block rows built from square stitches, after the knitted pattern: stepped diagonals, large symmetrical crosses, and figures drawn by the negative space. Stitch = 8 px; the row is 14 stitches high.

K1

Knit lozenge

from: Lozenge + stepped

The lozenge from the last sheet, drawn stitch by stitch: every diagonal becomes a staircase, and the action steps down to the lower half of the row.

K2

Cross index

from: Knit crosses

The index sits in a large symmetrical cross, like the stitched crosses in the knit. The title stays a plain bar; the action steps down and ends in a stepped point.

K3

Negative cross

from: Negative space in the knit

One continuous bar; the join between title and action is cut away as a stepped cross, so the paper draws the figure — the way the ground draws the peacocks.

K4

Facing pair

from: Mirrored figures

Symmetry instead of a drop: index and action are mirrored stepped wedges, both pointing in at the title, like the two birds facing the central stem.

K5

Crossed field

from: Cross field in the knit

The silhouette is a plain flat bar; the action cell is filled with small stitched crosses, taken from the peacock bodies. Texture marks the link instead of shape.

K6

Stepped hem

from: Knit border band

A flush bar with a hem of stepped teeth under the action only, like the knitted border band. The teeth point down toward the page.
