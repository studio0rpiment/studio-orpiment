Block row options

Eight alternatives to the dropped tab, each taken from a Song Cards border or arch. All keep the single flat colour and the index · title · action order.

Ref

Current

from: Reference

The dropped tab from the reference: reads as a lowercase h turned on its side.

01

Stepped

from: Geometric Kufic border

The title block descends to the action in three right-angle steps. Only horizontals and verticals; the stair points down and across toward the link.

02

Lancet

from: Pointed arch inner frame

The title block closes in a pointed arch laid on its side, and the action repeats the point at half height. The pointer is literal, the line stays single.

03

Ogee

from: Ogee / onion arch

The action stays attached but ends in an ogee point: two S-curves meeting at the link. A single ornamental moment on an otherwise square system.

04

Lozenge

from: Diamond interlace border

Each cell is cut on the √2 diagonal so the blocks nest into one another like a chevron; the action is a lozenge pointing on.

05

Meander edge

from: Geometric Kufic border

The dropped tab keeps its place but its upper edge becomes a squared meander, so the join reads as an ornamented band rather than a letterform.

06

Scalloped

from: Trefoil / multifoil arch

A flush action cell whose lower edge is a row of small lobes, from the multifoil arch. The row stays rectangular; only the hem is ornamented.

07

Vesica

from: Vesica piscis inner frame

The action detaches into an almond, pointed at both ends, set just below the row. Separate object, same colour.

08

Patterned cell

from: Hexagonal lattice border

No change of silhouette at all: one flat bar, and the action cell is marked by a fine hexagonal lattice instead of by its shape.
